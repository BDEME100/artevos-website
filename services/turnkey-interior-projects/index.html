Why turnkey usually costs less than managing it yourself

Running your own contractors looks cheaper on paper. In practice the cost lands in the gaps: the electrician who comes before the carpenter has finished, the tile that arrives short by six boxes, the fortnight lost waiting for a plumber. Turnkey removes those gaps because one schedule governs everyone.

You get one quotation, one timeline, one person to call, and one warranty covering the work.

What's included

Design & itemised budgetThe full design package plus a line-by-line budget you approve before anything starts.
Civil, electrical & plumbingWall changes, waterproofing, points, conduits, drainage and bathroom work.
Factory & site carpentryModular units built in a workshop; on-site joinery where the space needs it.
Painting & finishesPutty, primer, paint, texture and polish — with the surface prep that makes them last.
Vendor & schedule controlOne project manager sequencing every trade, with weekly progress updates.
Styling & handoverCurtains, lighting, decor, deep clean and a snag list closed before you move in.

How the money works

Payments are milestone-based, never everything up front:

StageWhat happensTypical share
BookingDesign starts, site measured, 3D begins10%
Design sign-off3D approved, materials frozen, production begins40%
Mid-executionCivil and carpentry substantially complete40%
HandoverSnags closed, styling done, keys returned10%

Exact percentages are confirmed in your written quotation before any payment is made. We can also connect you with no-cost and low-interest EMI options through finance partners.

Timeline

A typical 2–3 BHK turnkey project runs 8–16 weeks on site after design sign-off. Villas and duplexes with significant civil work run longer. You get a week-by-week schedule up front and a written update against it every week.

What is not included

So there are no surprises: structural changes needing society or authority approval, appliances and electronics, loose furniture you buy yourself, and any statutory deposits your apartment association charges for work. All of these are called out in the quotation.
