What full-home interior design actually means

Most homes in Bhubaneswar get decorated room by room, months apart, by whoever is free. The result is a house where nothing quite matches and storage runs out in year two. Full-home design solves the opposite way round: we plan every room together first — circulation, storage, electrical points, light, and the flow between spaces — and only then decide finishes.

That single decision is what makes a 2 BHK feel larger than its carpet area, keeps the pooja corner where it should be, and stops you discovering after handover that there is nowhere to keep suitcases.

How long it takes

Design typically takes 3–6 weeks from the first measurement to a signed-off 3D package. Execution runs 6–14 weeks depending on how much civil and carpentry work is involved. We share a week-by-week schedule before work starts and update you every week against it.

Rented, resale and phased homes

Not every project needs to be done at once. For rented and resale flats we lean on modular, reversible solutions; for owners on a tighter budget we phase the work room by room, starting with the kitchen and master bedroom, so the cost is spread over time without redesigning later.
